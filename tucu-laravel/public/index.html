Who We Are

A Community Built on the Word

The Tharaka University Christian Union is a vibrant fellowship of students, staff, and faculty who are committed to knowing Christ and making Him known on campus and beyond. We believe that faith and academic excellence go hand in hand.

Founded on the principles of Scripture, we provide a space for every student to encounter God, grow in discipleship, and serve their generation. Whether you're a committed believer or just beginning to explore faith — you belong here.
